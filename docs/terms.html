Terms of use

The plain-language terms for the Georgia Eats app and this website. Last updated 2026-10-26.

What the app is

Georgia Eats is a free guide to restaurants in Georgia. It is provided as is, for personal use, without charge and without warranties of any kind.

Check before you go

Restaurants open, close, change their hours and change their menus, and many plate-lunch counters and wing spots keep short or late hours. Dishes and founding years are what each place or a named source said when we checked in fall 2026. We work to keep the lists right, but we can't promise that any listing is current or complete. Call the restaurant before you make the trip.

Inspections and public records

The app and this site link each place to the Georgia Department of Public Health's inspection search (or GNR Public Health's, for Gwinnett, Newton and Rockdale); they show no inspection scores or letter grades and compute no grade. State alcohol license numbers and types, and whether a place is licensed to serve on the premises, come from the Georgia Department of Revenue's quarterly Active Alcohol Licenses report, as of the quarter shown in the app. For official records, use each agency's own site.
